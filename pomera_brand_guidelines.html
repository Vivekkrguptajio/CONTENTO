01 · Brand

An ad platform that sells certainty.

Pomera is an alternative ad platform for D2C brands in India. A brand commits a budget at a fixed price per 1,000 verified views. Independent publishers turn the brand's video into short vertical posts and publish them from their own accounts. Every view is verified, fraud is filtered out, and the brand is billed only for what was delivered.

The problem, on both sides

Brands feel uncertainty

A founder spending on Meta can't forecast what a rupee buys, can't explain results to investors and can't repeat a good month on purpose. The pain isn't the price. It's not knowing.

Publishers feel shut out

Skilled video editors can make content that performs, but creator platforms gate work by follower count. Their skill has no market.

How a campaign runs

  1. 1The brand commits a budget and a fixed rate per 1,000 verified views.
  2. 2The brand shares video (long-form, raw footage or product video) and guidelines.
  3. 3Publishers produce short vertical videos and post them from their own accounts.
  4. 4Every post is tracked. Views are verified and fraudulent traffic is filtered out.
  5. 5Publishers are paid per verified view, weekly, via UPI.
  6. 6The brand gets a report and is billed only for verified delivery.

Voice

Numbers first

"Spend ₹20,000. Get verified views. Pay only for delivery." Adjectives last.

Plain over clever

We sell accountability. Plain copy reads as true. Short sentences, active voice, second person.

Calm, not loud

Our buyer is anxious, not excited. No hype, no urgency tricks, no fake scarcity.

Specific over general

Name the rupee amount, the view count, the day of payout.

CTAs say what happens

"Start a campaign", "Join as a publisher", and on ClipperCircle "Join ClipperCircle". Never "Submit" or "Learn more".

Two registers

Brands: precise and accountable. Clippers on ClipperCircle: direct and money-first; Hinglish is fine on @clippercircle and WhatsApp.

03 · Colour

White and ink do the reading. Lime is the signal.

About 95% of any screen is white, paper and ink. Lime takes 5% or less: one band, one highlight, one live dot. Rationed, it reads as confident. Spread around, it reads as a promotion.

Lime rules

  • Lime is never text, on any background.
  • Lime is never a button. Primary buttons are ink on light and white on dark.
  • Lime never sits on white as a shape or mark (1.3:1). Use ink.
  • Anything on lime is ink: text, the Beam, buttons.
  • One lime moment per screen, and one lime tile in every nine Instagram posts.

Dark mode

Dark mode is the publisher side's home and an option everywhere else. Surfaces step up in lightness instead of using shadows. Buttons turn white. Lime stays a signal: the Beam, a live dot, the latest point on a chart. Never lime paragraphs on black: they glare and tire the eye.

04 · Typography

Geist for words. Geist Mono for numbers.

Headlines at 500, body at 400, nothing above 600. Confidence comes from space and precision, not weight.

The number rule

Every figure that carries meaning is set in Geist Mono with tabular figures: rupee amounts, view counts, CPM, dates, campaign IDs, payout status. It makes any Pomera screen recognisable without the logo, and it makes a campaign report read like a financial statement.

Rules

  • Sentence case for headings and buttons. Uppercase only for mono labels.
  • Indian number grouping for rupees and views: ₹2,50,000, 6,40,000.
  • Emphasis with weight 500, never italics or underline.
  • Headlines use text-wrap: balance. Body text stays under 680px wide.

No pill-shaped buttons or cards. Fully round is only for status dots and avatars.

Lines and elevation

  • Borders are 1px in the hairline colour. Use a 1px ink line only to open a table or a statement.
  • No shadows on cards or page sections. Shadow only on things that float: menus, popovers, modals, toasts.
  • In dark mode, depth comes from lighter surfaces (#0B0B0A → #141413 → #1C1C1A), not shadows.

Icons and motion

Icons

Lucide, 1.5px stroke, at 16, 20 or 24px, coloured like the text next to them. No filled or multi-colour icons, no emoji in the product.

Motion

120ms for hovers, 180ms for panels, easing cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1). Fades and small moves only. Turned off when the user prefers reduced motion.

07 · Tech handoff

Everything a developer needs.

Five files ship with this page: pomera-tokens.css, pomera-components.css, tailwind.config.js, tokens.json and the logo folder Pomera-Brand-Assets-v2. They are the source of truth; this page renders from the same code.

Setup

  1. 1Load the fonts (or self-host the .ttf files from 06-fonts-geist).
  2. 2Include pomera-tokens.css globally, before any component styles.
  3. 3Tailwind projects: use tailwind.config.js. Others: use the CSS variables directly.
  4. 4Style components only with --pm-c-* tokens so dark mode works automatically. Never hardcode a hex value.
  5. 5Dark mode follows the OS. Force it with data-theme="dark" or "light" on <html>.
  6. 6Copy 05-favicon into the site root and add the head tags below.

Accessibility checklist

  • Body text at least 4.5:1. Grey 500 is for labels only.
  • Visible focus on every interactive element. Never remove outlines.
  • Status shown with a dot and a word, never colour alone.
  • Touch targets at least 44 × 44px.
  • Every input has a visible label; errors are linked with aria-describedby.
  • Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
  • The logo has alt text "Pomera" (ClipperCircle lockup: "ClipperCircle by Pomera"); decorative Beams are aria-hidden.
